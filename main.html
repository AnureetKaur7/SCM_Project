    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    How does the Random Quote Generator work?

    The Random Quote Generator pulls from a large database of quotes. Each time you click the 'Generate Quote' button, a new, random quote is displayed for your inspiration.

    Do I need to create an account to use the generator?

    No, you can generate quotes without an account. However, creating an account allows you to save your favorite quotes and access additional features like personalized quote recommendations.

    Can I save quotes I like?

    Yes, by signing up and logging in, you can save quotes to your profile. You can access your saved quotes anytime in your personal collection.

    Can I share the quotes on social media?

    Yes! After generating a quote, you'll see options to share it directly on social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.

    How frequently is the quote database updated?

    Our quote database is regularly updated with new and diverse quotes from famous personalities, books, and other inspirational sources.

    Are there subscription plans?

    Yes, we offer both free and premium subscription plans. The premium plan provides additional features like access to exclusive quotes, an ad-free experience, and personalized recommendations.

    Can I request specific types of quotes?

    If you’re a premium member, you can select categories or themes for the quotes you want to generate, such as love, motivation, or wisdom.
